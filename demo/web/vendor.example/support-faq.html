Support FAQ

How long do we have to file a chargeback?

Chargebacks can be filed up to 120 days after the transaction date, or 120 days after the expected delivery date for goods and services that were not delivered.

Where do I find the settlement file?

On the SFTP server, folder /outbound/settlement, one file per business day.
